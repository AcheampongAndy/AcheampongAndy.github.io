Welcome

Andrews Acheampong

I work at the intersection of statistics, computational biology, and genomics.

I am an MPhil student in Biodata Analytics and Computational Genomics at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. My interests include Bayesian statistical methods, scalable MCMC, stochastic modelling, mathematical biology, computational statistics, and statistical genomics.

My current MPhil project investigates network-informed Bayesian shrinkage for ordinal biological outcomes, connecting high-dimensional genomic predictors with gene-network information.
